Learning data controls

Export creates a complete portable JSON backup of SEA browser-local LMS data, including Practical Logbook entries. Restore validates compatible complete backups and learner identity before replacing matching LMS data; older core-only backups remain supported. Practical Logbook entries also have separate JSON import and JSON/CSV export controls for logbook-only transfer.

Planned production LMS: secure server-side accounts, cross-device sync, full lesson completion, expanded quizzes, assignments, administrator reporting and certificates. The current browser-local features are intentionally transparent and do not claim centralized competency tracking.
