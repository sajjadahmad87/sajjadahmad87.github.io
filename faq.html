FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

About learning with Sajjad's Engineering Academy.

The academy is being developed as a free, practical engineering learning platform focused on real workplace knowledge.

Is the learning content free?

Yes. Public courses, engineering guides and technical resource-library pages are presented as free learning content.

Do I need an account to read the engineering guides?

No. Public engineering guides and resource-library pages can be read without signing in. A lightweight browser-local account may be used for selected download access while the production backend is being developed.

Are the full online courses already live?

The current site contains course roadmaps, previews and learning content. Interactive enrolment, saved progress, quizzes, assignments and certificate workflows are planned for the production LMS/backend phase.

Which engineering subjects are covered?

Current learning areas include HVAC and facilities, maintenance and reliability, PLC and automation, electrical engineering, steam and utilities, solar and energy, mechanical systems, industrial equipment and engineering management.

Can I use the technical resources as controlled procedures?

No. Academy content is for learning and professional development. Always verify actual work against the latest approved OEM manual, site SOP, permit-to-work requirements, LOTO rules, risk assessment and competent-person requirements.

Will certificates be available?

Completion certificates are planned as part of the future production learning system. A certificate should only be issued when the required learning and assessment process has been completed.

Can experienced engineers contribute courses?

Yes. The academy is designed to support practical knowledge-sharing by experienced engineering instructors. Use the Become an Instructor page to see the current instructor concept.

Where should I start?

Start with the free learning paths if you want structured study, the engineering guides for focused troubleshooting topics, the engineering calculators for practical calculations, or the technical resource hub for equipment-oriented reference material.

Still have a question?

Use the contact page for academy, technical-content or instructor enquiries.
