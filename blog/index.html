Maintenance & ReliabilityResearch Digest

Predictive Maintenance: Moving from Time-Based PPM to Condition-Based Decisions

Predictive maintenance combines condition data, failure history and analytical models to identify deterioration before functional failure.

Practical takeawayStart with clean failure modes and trustworthy sensor data. A sophisticated model cannot compensate for poor measurements, inconsistent tagging or weak maintenance history.

Reliability EngineeringResearch Digest

Machinery Health Prognostics and Remaining Useful Life

RUL methods aim to estimate how long a component can continue operating before its degradation reaches an unacceptable condition.

Practical takeawayUse RUL as a decision-support estimate—not an absolute promise. Confidence ranges, changing duty conditions and failure-mode shifts matter.

Motor faults can often be detected through vibration, motor current signature analysis, temperature and other condition indicators.

HVAC Fault Detection & Diagnostics for AHUs and Chilled-Water Systems

HVAC FDD research focuses on recognizing abnormal operating patterns early enough to reduce comfort issues, energy waste and equipment stress.

Practical takeawayUseful FDD begins with reliable sensors, correct sequences of operation and clear baseline behavior for temperatures, pressures, valve positions and airflow.

PLC & AutomationResearch Digest

PLC-Based Automation: Fault Diagnosis Beyond the Program Logic

Industrial automation faults frequently involve the boundary between PLC logic and real field devices: sensors, actuators, networks, drives and power supplies.

Practical takeawayTroubleshoot in layers: process condition → field input → PLC logic → output command → field response. This prevents random component replacement.
