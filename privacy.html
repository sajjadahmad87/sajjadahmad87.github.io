PRIVACY

Privacy Policy

This page explains what information the academy currently uses, where it is stored, and how the website is expected to evolve as account services are added.

CURRENT WEBSITE

Browser-local learner accounts

The current Sign Up and Sign In feature stores the learner profile and session in the visitor's own browser storage. The present static GitHub Pages version does not send that learner profile to a central SEA account database.

INFORMATION

What the sign-up form asks for

The current form may ask for name, email address, engineering discipline and country. These details are used only by the browser-local account experience unless the website is later upgraded to a server-backed account system.

CONTACT

Messages and enquiries

The current contact form prepares an email in the visitor's email application. Information sent by email is handled through the sender's and recipient's email services rather than through a website form database.

FUTURE SERVICES

Authentication and analytics

If SEA later introduces a hosted account database, analytics, newsletters, payment services or private file storage, this policy should be updated before those services are activated to explain the information collected, purposes, providers, retention and user choices.

YOUR CHOICES

Managing current browser data

You can sign out using the site controls. Because the current account is stored locally, browser/site-data controls can also remove the saved account information. Clearing site storage may require you to sign up again on that browser.

For privacy questions or requests concerning information you have directly sent to the academy, contact info@sajjadengineeringacademy.com.

Policy status: This policy describes the website's current static implementation. It should be reviewed whenever SEA adds central user accounts, analytics, marketing tools, payments or other third-party data processing.
