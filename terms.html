TERMS OF USE

Use the academy as a learning platform.

These terms describe the intended use of Sajjad's Engineering Academy and its free technical learning material.

Last updated: 15 August 2026

Learning purpose

The academy provides educational and professional-development content. It does not replace employer procedures, competent-person decisions, statutory requirements, OEM instructions or site-specific engineering controls.

Free access

Public courses, guides and resource-library pages are currently presented as free learning content. Future platform features may be introduced separately and these terms may be updated if the service model changes.

Technical safety

Engineering work can involve electrical, mechanical, pressure, thermal, chemical and other hazards. Before performing any task, users must follow applicable laws, approved procedures, PTW/LOTO requirements, risk assessments, manufacturer instructions and competent supervision.

Accuracy and updates

Reasonable care is taken to organize useful technical learning material, but equipment specifications, standards, procedures and site conditions can change. Users should verify important information against current controlled sources before applying it.

Intellectual property and source material

Academy-created explanations and layouts may be used for personal learning and professional development. Third-party manuals, trademarks, images, specifications and other source material remain subject to their respective owners' rights and conditions.

Platform previews

Dashboard statistics, enrolment controls, progress tracking, certificates and other LMS functions may be shown as interface previews until the production backend is connected. Preview functions should not be treated as completed records or issued credentials.

Acceptable use

Do not misuse the website, attempt unauthorized access, distribute malicious code, falsely represent academy credentials, or use learning material in a way that bypasses required safety or legal controls.

Changes

The academy may improve its content, structure and platform features over time. Material changes to these terms should be reflected on this page.

Questions

For questions about these terms, use the contact page.
