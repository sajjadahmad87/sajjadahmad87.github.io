ABOUT THE ACADEMY

Closing the gap between theory and the plant floor.

Sajjad's Engineering Academy is an independent knowledge platform created to make industrial engineering concepts clearer, more practical and easier to apply at work.

OUR MISSION

Make engineering knowledge actionable.

We turn complex maintenance, reliability, utilities and management subjects into structured learning that technicians and engineers can use to inspect, troubleshoot and improve industrial systems.

OUR VISION

Better decisions. Safer plants. Stronger teams.

We aim to grow a trusted practical-learning platform where engineering professionals develop sound judgement, communicate with evidence and create measurable operational value.

HOW WE TEACH

A practical learning method.

Content follows the way strong engineers approach work: establish facts, understand the system, identify causes and verify results.

STEP 01

Understand the system

Start with purpose, components, operating sequence and normal parameters.

STEP 02

Observe the evidence

Use readings, alarms, history, inspection findings and operator information.

STEP 03

Test the causes

Separate symptoms from root causes and challenge assumptions systematically.

STEP 04

Control and improve

Implement corrective action, assign ownership and confirm effectiveness.

ACADEMY PRINCIPLES

What every resource should deliver.

  • Practical relevance to industrial work
  • Clear technical language and structure
  • Evidence-led troubleshooting
  • Safety and professional responsibility
  • Measurable maintenance improvement
  • Respect for OEM and site requirements
The academy currently operates as an independent educational-content platform. It does not claim KHDA or governmental accreditation and does not currently issue regulated qualifications.
