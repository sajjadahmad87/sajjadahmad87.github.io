PLC Troubleshooting for Industrial Maintenance

Test your ability to trace evidence through inputs, logic, outputs and field devices without unsafe forcing, bypassing or uncontrolled program changes. Assessment attempts are stored only on this browser.

Beginner → Intermediate → Advanced progression

Prerequisites: basic PLC, sensor and actuator terminology plus awareness that approved access, PTW/LOTO, risk assessment, backups, change control and competent-person controls govern real work.

  1. Beginner—learn the evidence model: study the four-layer PLC troubleshooting model.
  2. Intermediate—trace and record the control path: follow the eight-stage workflow, use the blank PLC fault evidence worksheet and study the curated external PLC video pathway.
  3. Advanced—test diagnostic decisions: complete the knowledge check, open the exact review lesson for each missed answer and retry when ready.

Learning outcomes: distinguish controller indication from field state, preserve evidence before reset, trace inputs and outputs logically, respect safety/change-control boundaries and verify the complete machine response after repair.

Study before retrying

Use the academy guide, blank evidence worksheet and curated external lecture series to reinforce the complete control-path method.
